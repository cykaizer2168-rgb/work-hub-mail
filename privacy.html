Work Hub Mail privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Who uses it

Work Hub Mail is a personal tool used by one person, its owner, on their own Mac. It is not a public service and does not accept other users.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's consent, through the Gmail API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify, it reads messages in the owner's inbox, marks messages read or unread, archives threads, downloads attachments the owner opens, and sends replies the owner writes and confirms. It also reads the signed-in account's email address to check that the right account was connected.

How the data is used

Mail is shown to the owner on their Mac. Nothing is sent unless the owner clicks Send on a reply. When the owner clicks Summarize or Draft reply, the text of that one thread is sent to the AI assistant the owner chose (Anthropic Claude or OpenAI Codex), under the owner's own account, to produce text that is shown back to the owner. No other use is made of the data.

Storage

Mail is not copied to any database; it is read from Gmail when the page is opened. The sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain on the owner's Mac. Disconnecting the account in the app deletes the token, and access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, except for the thread text the owner chooses to send to an AI assistant as described above. Work Hub Mail's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
