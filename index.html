Work Hub Mail

A private mail page for one person, running on their own Mac.

Work Hub Mail shows the owner's own Gmail inboxes on one page so they can answer customer support email from a single screen. It runs only on the owner's Mac and is not offered to anyone else.

With the owner's permission it reads their inbox, marks messages read, archives threads, and sends a reply only when the owner clicks Send.
